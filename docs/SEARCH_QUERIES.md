# Search Queries

The search queries collect literature to cover the following topic areas and 
five habitats as the following extract from 
[About the German Biodiversity Assessment](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/) describes:

_**What is the structure of the assessment?** For the most important habitats 
in Germany ([agricultural and open land](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/agricultural-open-land), 
[forests](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/forest/), 
[inland waters and floodplains](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/inland-waters-floodplains/), 
[coasts and coastal waters](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/coasts-coastal-waters/), 
and [urban areas](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/urban-areas/)), 
the following questions will be answered: What are the current 
[trends and direct drivers](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/trends-direct-drivers/) 
of biodiversity change? What are the [consequences](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/consequences/) 
of species loss for our society? Which [measures](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/measures/) 
for biodiversity protection and increase do really work? What role do 
[indirect drivers](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/indirect-drivers/) 
play? What is the special role of [soil biodiversity](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/soil-biodiversity/)? 
How do we need to [transform](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/transformationpotential/) 
our lifestyles, economies, and land use to maintain biodiversity or bring it 
back to our landscapes? This results in a matrix structure of chapter groups 
for the 5 habitats listed above and cross-cutting topic areas._

Topic Areas:
- [Trends & direct drivers of biodiversity change](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/trends-direct-drivers/)
- [Consequences of current biodiversity changes for ecosystems and society](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/consequences/)
- [Effectiveness of measures and implementation obstacles](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/measures/)
- [Indirect drivers](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/indirect-drivers/)
- [Soil Biodiversity](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/soil-biodiversity/)
- [Transformation potentials for biodiversity conservation](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/topic-areas/transformationpotential/)

Habitats:
- [Agricultural and Open Land](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/agricultural-open-land/)
- [Forest](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/forest/)
- [Inland Waters and Floodplains](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/inland-waters-floodplains/)
- [Coasts and Coastal Waters](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/coasts-coastal-waters/)
- [Urban Areas](https://www.feda.bio/en/about-faktencheck-artenvielfalt/habitats/urban-areas/)

> **Note 1**: The search queries replicates the queries documented in the 
[Search Strings](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eOERoaJ4nrdC8TiU65rP7i3W8r2P6yE5sQtGS2Sha9s/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.bp1imz678xa) document.
> **Note 2**: The assumption is that the string queries should cover the combination of topic arenas and habitats.

Biodiversität AND Deutschland AND Lebensraum AND Themenbereich.
